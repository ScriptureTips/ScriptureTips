No account or login needed. 1) Install the extension. 2) Open https://www.adonai-reigns.life/demo/jonah.html (a demo blog page with no scripture script of its own). 3) References such as "John 3:16" become links with a small logo; hover or tap one to see the passage in a tooltip (pin button keeps it open). 4) Click the toolbar button: the switch turns it off for the site; "Generator" and "Settings" open the other pages. The passage text is fetched from scripture.tips.
