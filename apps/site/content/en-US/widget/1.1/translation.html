Your site's translation

Set translation to the ID of the translation passages should open in:

ScriptureTips.run("main", { translation: "bsb" });

Without it, passages open in the King James Version (eng-kjv). The IDs are listed at {{data}}/api/widget/translations.

For one passage

Write the translation's abbreviation in brackets straight after the reference, as in “John 3:16 (LEB)”, and that passage opens in it. An abbreviation the plugin doesn't have is ignored, and the passage opens as usual.

Your visitor's choice

A visitor can change the translation in the tooltip. That choice belongs to that link and is kept in their browser.

If your site has accounts and knows which translation a visitor prefers, tell the plugin with setPreferences.

Which wins

  1. What the visitor chose in that link's tooltip.
  2. The translation a link's own address names (t=), for a link you wrote to the ScriptureTips reader.
  3. An abbreviation written after the reference.
  4. The visitor's preference, from setPreferences.
  5. Your translation option.
  6. The King James Version.
