What is recognized

Book names in full or abbreviated, with a chapter, a verse or a range: “Psalm 23”, “Jn 3:16”, “Romans 5:8–10”, “1 Cor 13:4-7”.

What is always left alone

  • Links you wrote yourself. Nothing is ever linked twice.
  • code, pre, kbd and samp.
  • Form fields and anything editable.
  • Scripts and styles.

A reference that is split across elements, such as John <b>3</b>:16, isn't found.

Leaving more out

Put the class st-ignore or the attribute data-st-ignore on any element, and nothing inside it is linked:

<p class="st-ignore">Our address is 3:16 John Street.</p>

Or name what to skip with the ignore option, a CSS selector:

ScriptureTips.run("main", { ignore: "nav, .byline, h1" });

Verse numbering

References are read in the King James Version's numbering unless you say otherwise. If your writing follows another tradition's numbering (in the Hebrew numbering Joel 2:28 is Joel 3:1, for instance), set scheme and the right verses are shown in every translation:

ScriptureTips.run("main", { scheme: "hebrew" });

The schemes are kjv, hebrew, latin and greek.
