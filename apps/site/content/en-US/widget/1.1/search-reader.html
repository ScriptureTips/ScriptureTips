What picking a passage opens

When a visitor picks a passage in the results, the search's results make way for a reader, in the same place on your page:

  • A blue bar with the book and chapter, and arrows to the previous and next chapter (they go across books).
  • A book picker: the book's name in the bar opens the list of the translation's books, under Old Testament, New Testament and Other. The chapter number opens that book's chapters.
  • A translation picker under the bar, for the translations you offer (data-translations). Changing it keeps the place.
  • The passage, with the translation's credit under it. For a part of a chapter, Read in context adds a few verses each side, and Read the whole chapter shows all of it.
  • Back to the results returns to the results. Typing or searching again does the same.

It reads from the data server only, with no cookies. To open the passage somewhere else instead (your own reading page), give your own callback; then the reader isn't used.
