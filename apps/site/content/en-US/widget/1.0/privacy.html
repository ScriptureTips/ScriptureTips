The plugin is built so that adding it changes nothing about your site's privacy position. The full privacy policy covers the service.

What it sends

  • Passage requests only. When a visitor opens a tooltip, their browser asks {{dataHost}} for that passage: the reference and the translation. It also asks once for the list of translations.
  • No cookies, sent or set.
  • Nothing about the page: not its address, its text or the visitor. As with any request a page makes, the browser names your site's domain and the server sees the visitor's IP address.

Until a visitor opens a tooltip, nothing is requested: finding the references happens in their browser.

What it keeps

One thing: the translation a visitor chose for a link, in their own browser (localStorage, under scripturetips:translations), written only when they choose. It is never sent anywhere, and at most the latest 500 choices are kept. Set the storage option to null to keep nothing beyond the page.

Its weight

One script, under 45 KB before compression, with no dependencies and no fonts, images or stylesheets of its own.
