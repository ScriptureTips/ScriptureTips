The website plugin gives every visitor to your site what the browser extension gives its users. It finds the scripture references in your pages, makes them links, and shows the passage in a tooltip when a visitor points at one or taps it.

Why use it

  • Nothing to maintain: you write “John 3:16” as plain text, in a new article or one from years ago, and it is linked.
  • Light: one small script with no dependencies, which loads nothing else until a visitor opens a passage.
  • Private: no cookies and no tracking, so it needs no consent banner.
  • Free: no charge, no advertising, no account.
