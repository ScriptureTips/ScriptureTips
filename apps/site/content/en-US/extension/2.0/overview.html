Scripture Tips is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. On the pages you read, it finds scripture references such as “John 3:16” or “Rom 5:8–10”, makes them links, and shows the passage when you point at one.

What you can do with it

  • Read a passage without leaving the page, and open the whole chapter with Read more.
  • Choose the translation for each passage, or for all of them.
  • Keep a passage open while you write a reply.
  • Link the references in your own writing before you post it.
  • Send links to another Bible site if you prefer one.
  • Turn it off on any site where you don't want it.

What it costs

Nothing, ever. ScriptureTips has no advertising, no subscriptions and no accounts to buy. What the extension sends and keeps says exactly what leaves your browser.
