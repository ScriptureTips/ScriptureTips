Scripture on every page you read

ScriptureTips recognizes scripture references such as John 3:16 wherever they are written, makes them links, and shows the passage when you point at one, in the translation you choose.

{{dataHost}} is the service behind it. If a link brought you here, this is where its passage came from.

For the pages you read

The Scripture Tips browser extension for Chrome and Firefox links the references on any website, and comes with a link generator for the things you write.

Get the extension · How it works

For your own website

The website plugin is one script. Add it to your site and your visitors see each passage without leaving your page, whether or not they have the extension.

Add the plugin · How it works

For reading and study

Whole chapters, several translations side by side, and the Hebrew and Greek behind them are on the ScriptureTips reader, at {{siteHost}}. Read more in a passage's tooltip opens it there.
