Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

This policy covers the ScriptureTips data service ({{dataHost}}), the Scripture Tips browser extension and the ScriptureTips website plugin. The ScriptureTips reader ({{siteHost}}) has its own policy. ScriptureTips follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation for people in Europe.

What we collect

There are no accounts here, so we don't ask for your name, email address or anything else about you. We don't use cookies, advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Like every web server, ours record each request: your IP address, the time, the address asked for and your browser's name. We use these records only to keep the service running and secure, and delete them once they are no longer needed for that.

When you point at a reference, the browser extension or the website plugin asks {{dataHost}} for that passage. The request carries only the reference and the translation: not the page you're on, and no other text from it. When the website plugin asks, your browser also names the website you are on, as it does for every request a page makes.

The help's search runs in your browser. What you type into it is not sent to us.

What stays in your browser

The translation you last chose for each link is kept in your own browser and never sent to us.

The extension keeps its settings in your browser's storage. If you've turned on your browser's sync, your browser's maker (such as Mozilla or Google) carries them between your devices under its own privacy policy.

Where it's kept

Our servers are run by netcup GmbH, a German company, in the European Union. We don't sell, share or give your information to anyone, except where the law requires it.
