## What it says

- **Copyright Policy:** contributors may share only public-domain work, or copyrighted work under a free-culture, open-content license: CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY 4.0 or CC0 (fair use only in exceptional cases). The project follows US copyright law and doesn't honor claims over public-domain works. Faithful reproductions of public-domain works (transcriptions, facsimiles) are considered public domain. Works published before 1 January 1931 are public domain (the JPS 1917 text qualifies; see `../jps-tanakh-1917/`).
- **Terms of Use:** the project is a nonprofit, non-commercial, free-of-charge service. Contributors must license content so it is freely reusable; public-domain works must be attributed with their source and any modifications noted.
- **Home page:** almost every resource not in the public domain is shared under an open license, with attribution as the core requirement.
